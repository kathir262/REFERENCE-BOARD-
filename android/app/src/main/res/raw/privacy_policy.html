RefPad Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026

RefPad is a free app for artists: a reference board, timed gesture practice, a PDF reader and a floating reference window. It has no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no analytics.

What RefPad collects

Nothing. RefPad does not collect, sell or share any personal data or usage data. The app does not have the Internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your device.

Your images and PDFs

When you add images or PDFs, RefPad keeps a copy in its own private storage on your device, so your board, practice list and reading position are still there next time. These copies never leave your device and are not included in Android cloud backups. Removing items, clearing the board or uninstalling RefPad deletes them.

How files reach RefPad

You choose files with Android's own photo picker or file picker, or share them to RefPad from another app. RefPad cannot browse your storage, photos, camera, microphone, location or contacts.

Clipboard

Only when you tap Paste and RefPad has nothing copied itself, it reads an image from your clipboard to place it on the board. Nothing else is read from the clipboard.

Permissions

Display over other apps — shows the floating reference window on top of your drawing app. It is only used when you turn the floating window on.
Foreground service — keeps the floating window running while you draw, with a notification you can use to close it.

Children

RefPad is suitable for everyone and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be shown in the app and on RefPad's Google Play page, with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Use the developer email address shown on RefPad's Google Play page.
