RefPad Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026

Free · No ads · No tracking · No data collected

RefPad is a free app for artists on Android and Windows: a reference board, timed gesture practice, a PDF reader and a floating reference window. It has no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no analytics.

What RefPad collects

Nothing. RefPad does not collect, sell or share any personal data or usage data. On Android the app does not have the Internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your device. On Windows the only connection RefPad makes is the optional update check described below.

Your images and PDFs

When you add images or PDFs, RefPad keeps a copy in its own private storage on your device, so your board, practice list and reading position are still there next time. These copies never leave your device and are not included in Android cloud backups. Removing items, clearing the board or uninstalling RefPad deletes them.

How files reach RefPad

You choose files with Android's own photo picker or file picker, or share them to RefPad from another app. RefPad cannot browse your storage, photos, camera, microphone, location or contacts.

Clipboard

Only when you tap Paste and RefPad has nothing copied itself, it reads an image from your clipboard to place it on the board. Nothing else is read from the clipboard.

Permissions

  • Display over other apps — shows the floating reference window on top of your drawing app. It is only used when you turn the floating window on.
  • Foreground service — keeps the floating window running while you draw, with a notification you can use to close it.

RefPad for Windows

  • Your files stay on your PC. Pictures you add to the board are copied into RefPad's own folder (%LOCALAPPDATA%\RefPad) so the board keeps working if the originals move. PDFs and practice folders are opened where they are. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Update check. At most once a day, and when you click Check for updates, RefPad asks GitHub (github.com, where RefPad is published) whether a newer version exists, and downloads it only if you choose to update. The request contains no personal information and nothing about your files; like any website, GitHub can see your IP address (see GitHub's privacy statement). You can turn automatic checks off in About RefPad.
  • Clipboard. RefPad reads the clipboard only when you paste, and writes to it only when you copy.
  • Removing your data. Clear the board and practice list in the app, or delete %LOCALAPPDATA%\RefPad. Uninstalling removes the program but keeps that folder until you delete it.

Children

RefPad is suitable for everyone and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy
