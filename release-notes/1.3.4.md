RefPad 1.3.4 for Android

- Name your board: tap the board name at the top left → Name this board / Rename board. A new board asks for a name.
- Move the toolbar: drag it by the dotted handle to the left side, the right side or the bottom (or choose in Settings → Toolbar).
- Hide the toolbar: tap Hide and it becomes a small circle. Tap the circle to bring it back; long-press it for every tool.
- Shorter long-press menus without the toolbar's buttons (they appear as a row of icons while the toolbar is hidden).
