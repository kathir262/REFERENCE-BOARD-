RefPad 1.3.5 for Windows

- Name your board: click the board name at the top left → Name this board / Rename board (or press F2). Renaming also renames its file. A new board asks for a name.
- Settings (⋮ → Settings, or Ctrl+,): updates, board background, tool bar position, chime, memory mode, practice history and About in one place.
- When an update is ready, the ⋮ button gets a yellow dot, Settings is highlighted, and RefPad offers to update.
- Move the tool bar: drag it by its dotted handle to the left side, the right side or the bottom (or click the handle, or choose in Settings).
- Hide the tool bar: click Hide and it folds into a small round button. Click the button to bring the bar back; right-click it for every tool.
- Shorter right-click menus without the tool bar's buttons (they appear as a row of icons while the bar is folded).
- Practice: right-click a pose → Add this pose to the board.
- PDF: right-click a page → add it to the board or practise from it, right at the top of the menu.
- A PDF of up to 30 pages added to the board goes straight onto the board, every page; longer ones still let you pick the pages.
