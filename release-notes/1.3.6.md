RefPad 1.3.6 for Windows

- Zoom keys work on every tab, the same as in drawing apps: Ctrl and + or =, the + and = keys, and the number-pad + to zoom in; Ctrl and -, the - key and the number-pad - to zoom out. Practice now zooms with them too.
- Two-finger pinch zooms on the Board, in the PDF reader and in Practice (touch screens, pen displays and touchpads).
- Fixed: in the floating window, after the pen or mouse passed over the window's edge, the resize arrow stayed on the whole window. It now only shows on the edge itself.
