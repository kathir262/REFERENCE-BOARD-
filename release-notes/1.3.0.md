RefPad 1.3.0 – lots of new tools for studying your references.

Board
- Undo and redo for everything (Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y)
- Crop a picture right on the board (C), lock it in place (L), add notes (N)
- Value steps (2–5 greys), squint blur, a grid over a picture, opacity
- Colour picker (I): copies the colour code
- Pin a picture on top of other apps, export the board as a PNG (Ctrl+E)
- Dark, neutral grey or light background
- Drag pictures straight from a web page onto the board

Practice
- Timed classes: warm-up, 30 or 60 minutes, or your own plan
- Memory mode: the pose hides when its time is up, draw it from memory
- Practice history with your poses, minutes and streak

PDF reader
- Two pages side by side and a page list with thumbnails
- Bookmarks (Ctrl+D) and text search (Ctrl+F)
- Snip (S): drag a box around part of a page to put just that on the board

Your board and settings are kept when you update.
