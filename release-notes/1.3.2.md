RefPad 1.3.2 for Android

- Adding a PDF to the board now puts its pages straight onto the board as pictures, and you stay on the board.
- A long book (more than 30 pages) asks first: all pages, the first 20, page numbers you type, or pick pages by looking at them.
- Includes the 1.3.1 fix for the PDF reader closing when a PDF was opened.

Your board and settings are kept when you update.
